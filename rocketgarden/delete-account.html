Delete your Rocket Garden account

Rocket Garden: Space Farm Idle, by Aloha Joe's Apps.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Rocket Garden, go to the More tab and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and cloud save right away and resets the game on that device.

Without the game

Email joeaisinger@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Rocket Garden account", from the email address of the Google or Apple account you linked (or include your friend code from the game's profile). We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account ID, linked sign-in information and your cloud save (colonies, crops, rockets, artifacts, research, currencies, stats, settings). Purchase records kept by Google Play or Apple are managed by those stores. Anonymous analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase within 14 months.

Common questions

How do I learn the game?

Sprout walks you through your first session. You can replay the opening story any time in More → Settings → Replay the opening.

How do I keep my colonies on a new phone?

Link your account in the game's profile with Google or Apple on the old phone, then sign in with the same account on the new one.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Open More → Shop and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the store receipt.

A reward video didn't give me anything.

Rewards come when the video finishes. If it finished and nothing arrived, email us the time and what you were watching it for.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: More → Profile → Delete account, or use the account deletion page.

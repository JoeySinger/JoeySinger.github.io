Privacy Policy — Koi Pond Idle

Effective date: November 4, 2026. Developer: Aloha Joe's Apps ("we"). Contact: joeaisinger@gmail.com.

Koi Pond Idle: Merge Fish ("the game") is an idle merge game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is meant for players aged 13 and older.

Information the game collects

  • Game progress: your koi, ponds, research, currencies, boosts, stats and settings. It is stored on your device and, so you don't lose it, in our cloud database (Google Firebase Cloud Firestore) linked to an account ID.
  • Account information: when you first open the game, a random anonymous account ID is created (Firebase Authentication). If you choose to link Google or Apple, we receive that account's identifier and, if provided, your name and email address, only to recognize you when you sign in again.
  • Purchases: Google Play and the Apple App Store process all payments. We never see your card or bank details. We receive a record of which items you bought so we can deliver and restore them.
  • Usage and diagnostics: Firebase Analytics records gameplay events (for example a new pond reached, a release, a boost started, a video watched, and how long you have played) with device type, OS version, approximate region and an app-instance identifier. Firebase Crashlytics records crash reports. We use this to tune the game's pacing and fix bugs.
  • Advertising: the game offers optional reward videos through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads and, if you allow it, to personalize them.

How we use it

To run the game and save your progress; to deliver purchases; to understand how the game is played and fix problems; to show the videos you choose to watch; and to send reminders you have turned on (pond full, offline limit, daily gift). Reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not send push messages from a server.

Advertising choices and consent

  • Where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before showing personalized ads. Where it applies, you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iOS, the game shows Apple's tracking permission shortly after you start playing. If you decline, ads are not personalized using the advertising identifier. You can change this in your device's Settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that run the game: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and AdMob) and, when you buy something or link an account, Google Play or Apple. Their use is governed by their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy, Apple Privacy Policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Progress is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and cloud save at any time in the game (Settings → Delete account) or on our account deletion page. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each player's cloud save can be read and written only by that player's account.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to some uses. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

Email us at joeaisinger@gmail.com with your device and what happened. We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

How do I learn the game?

Open Settings → How to play to read any part of the tutorial or replay it.

How do I keep my pond on a new phone?

Link your account in the game's profile with Google or Apple on the old phone, then sign in with the same account on the new one.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases (it's also in Settings). If that doesn't help, email us with the store receipt.

A reward video didn't give me anything.

Rewards come when the video finishes. If it finished and nothing arrived, email us the time and what you were watching it for.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings → Delete account, or use the account deletion page.

Haven privacy policy

Haven, by Aloha Joe's Apps. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Haven is a cozy habit tracker. This page explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Haven is for people aged 13 and over. Questions: joeaisinger@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account

When you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we store an account ID and the email address your sign-in provider gives us (Apple may give a private relay address). If you use email and password, Firebase Authentication stores a hashed password; we never see it.

Your habits and haven

Your habits (names, icons, schedules, notes), check-ins, focus sessions, life-area progress, acorns, streaks, decorations, haven layout and companions are stored in your account so they sync between your devices.

Mood check-ins

If you use the mood check-in, your mood, optional tags and optional note are stored privately in your account. They are never shown to friends and never sent to analytics.

Health data (optional)

If you turn on "Complete automatically from Health" for a habit, Haven reads steps, workouts and exercise minutes, sleep and water from Apple Health or Android Health Connect (and mindful minutes from Apple Health). Haven only reads this data. It is used on your device to mark that habit complete. The health values themselves are not uploaded to our servers, are not used for advertising, and are never shared with anyone. You can turn this off at any time in the habit or in your phone's Health settings.

Friends and circles

If you add friends, they can see your display name, friend code, companion, haven, life-area levels and visit streak. Habit names stay private unless you choose to make a habit visible. Social messages are preset cheers, stickers and gifts; there is no free-text chat.

Purchases

Haven Plus purchases are handled by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. We receive your subscription status and an anonymous purchase ID. We never receive your card details.

Device and usage data

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn which features are used and to fix crashes. Events describe actions such as "check-in in the Body area"; they never include habit names, notes or mood values. You can turn analytics off at any time in You → Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage". A push-notification token is stored so friends' cheers and your reminders can reach your device.

Pictures of your haven

Photo mode and the monthly recap make pictures on your device. They are only shared when you choose to share them, through your phone's own share sheet; Haven does not upload them.

How we use it

  • To run the app and sync your haven between devices.
  • To deliver the social features you choose to use.
  • To unlock Haven Plus when you buy it.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your data. Haven has no ads and no advertising SDKs.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, messaging, analytics, crash reports).
  • RevenueCat (subscription status).
  • Apple and Google (sign-in and purchases).

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app (You → Settings → Account → Delete account) or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase within 14 months.

Children

Haven is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Email us and we will help within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update this page and note it in the app.

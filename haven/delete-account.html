Delete your Haven account

Haven, by Aloha Joe's Apps.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Haven and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm, then sign in once more when asked.

This deletes your account and all its data right away, and signs you out on every device.

Without the app

Email joeaisinger@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Haven account", from the email address of the Apple, Google or email account you signed in with (or include your friend code). We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account ID and sign-in information, habits, check-ins, mood check-ins, focus sessions, life-area progress, acorns, decorations, haven layout, companions, public profile, friend code, friendships, circle memberships, league entries, messages you sent, and your device's notification tokens.

What is kept

A marker with your account ID is kept for 30 days so your other devices can sign out safely; it is then removed. Purchase records kept by Google Play or Apple are managed by those stores. Anonymous analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase within 14 months.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Haven Plus subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Common questions

My progress isn't on my new phone

Sign in with the same method (Apple, Google or email) you used before. Your haven syncs automatically.

I bought Haven Plus but it isn't unlocked

Go to You → Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with.

How do I cancel Haven Plus?

Go to You → Settings → Manage Haven Plus, or open your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

I had a quiet few days. Did I lose anything?

No. Haven never takes anything away. Your haven keeps everything, and one check-in wakes a sleepy area right up.

Someone is bothering me

Open their profile in Circles and tap Block or Report. Blocked people cannot send you anything.

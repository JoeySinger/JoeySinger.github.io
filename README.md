Each later game (Koi Pond, Dye Garden) gets its own folder next to `fireflyhollow/` and a card on the home page; `app-ads.txt` stays at the root and covers every app.

Before production: put your AdMob publisher ID in `app-ads.txt`, and replace `[DATE OF FIRST RELEASE]` in the privacy policy and terms.

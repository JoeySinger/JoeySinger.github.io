Delete your Firefly Hollow account

Firefly Hollow: Color Sort, by Aloha Joe's Apps.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Firefly Hollow and tap the gear for Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and cloud save right away and resets the game on that device.

Without the game

Email joeaisinger@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Firefly Hollow account", from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with (or include your player name). We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account ID, linked sign-in information and your cloud save (progress, coins, boosters, stats, settings). Purchase records kept by Google Play or Apple are managed by those stores. Anonymous analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase within 14 months.

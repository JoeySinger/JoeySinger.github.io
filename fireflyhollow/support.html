Firefly Hollow Support

Email us at support@alohajoesapps.com. In the game, Settings → Contact support opens an email with your app version and device already filled in. We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Settings → Account and sign in with Google or Apple on the old phone, then sign in with the same account on the new one.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the store receipt.

A level seems impossible.

Every level is checked by a solver and can be won without boosters. Try the Lantern booster for a hint, and remember Undo.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account, or use the account deletion page.
